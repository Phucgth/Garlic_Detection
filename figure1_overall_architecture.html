Fig. 1. Overall architecture of the proposed EfficientNetB4-FSDA model for garlic disease classification. The pipeline processes an input image (380 × 380 × 3) through an EfficientNetB4 backbone (Blocks 1–2 frozen, Blocks 3–7 fine-tuned, batch normalization always frozen), followed by the proposed Frequency-Spatial Dual Attention (FSDA) block, a global average pooling layer, and a fully-connected classification head with Dropout regularization and Softmax output.
