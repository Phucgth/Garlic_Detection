Fig. 2. Internal architecture of the proposed Frequency-Spatial Dual Attention (FSDA) block. The input feature map (12 × 12 × 1792) is processed by two parallel branches: (1) the Frequency Channel Attention branch applies a 2D FFT, log-magnitude, global average pooling, and a bottleneck MLP (FC1 + ReLU → FC2 + Sigmoid) to produce channel-wise attention weights; (2) the Spatial Attention branch applies channel-wise average/max pooling, concatenation, and a Conv 7×7 + Sigmoid to produce a spatial attention map (also used for visualization). The two attended feature maps are fused via element-wise addition followed by Batch Normalization, yielding an attention-enhanced feature map of the same shape (12 × 12 × 1792).
